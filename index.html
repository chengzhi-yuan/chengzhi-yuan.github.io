About Me

I am an Associate Professor and Graduate Director in the Department of Mechanical, Industrial & Systems Engineering at the University of Rhode Island, where I direct the Intelligent Control and Robotics Laboratory. I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from North Carolina State University in 2016, with a minor in Mathematics.

My research lies broadly in dynamical systems and control, with a particular emphasis on analytical and provable learning methods for uncertain systems. I am interested in developing learning mechanisms whose behavior can be understood and certified mathematically, and in bringing these methods to robotic and autonomous systems.

Research Interests

  • Analytical and provable adaptive learning methods for system modeling, diagnosis, and control

  • Hybrid systems, switching control, and impulsive control

  • Multi-agent distributed learning and control

  • Applications to multi-robot coordination, soft robotics, autonomous underwater vehicles, and biomedical signal processing
