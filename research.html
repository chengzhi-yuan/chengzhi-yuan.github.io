Research

My research focuses on learning and control of uncertain dynamical systems, with an emphasis on methods that admit rigorous analysis and performance guarantees. Current and continuing directions include:

Provable Adaptive Learning and Control

We develop analytical learning methods that identify useful dynamical information online and use that information for modeling, fault diagnosis, and feedback control. A recurring goal is to connect learning capability with mathematically verifiable excitation, stability, and convergence properties.

Multi-Agent Distributed Learning and Control

We study cooperative learning, estimation, and control in networked dynamical systems, including coordination of heterogeneous agents and multi-robot systems under uncertainty and limited information exchange.

Hybrid, Switched, and Time-Delay Systems

Our work also addresses hybrid and switched control, impulsive systems, actuator constraints, and time-delay systems, including analysis and synthesis methods based on switching, dynamic IQCs, and related tools.

Robotics and Autonomous Systems

Application areas include autonomous underwater vehicles, soft robots, multi-robot coordination, robotic manipulation, and intelligent sensing and diagnosis.
